ii

Axis calibration

Two ways to calibrate. Manual is the most accurate for tricky charts.

◉ Axis-frame — drag two perpendicular lines

Drag the ⬛ origin handle onto where the axes meet, the ● X-end handle to a known X-axis tick, and the ● Y-end handle to a known Y-axis tick. The horizontal and vertical lines stay locked to the origin so they always cross perfectly.

Enter the data values at each handle

Tip: arrow keys nudge the selected handle by 1 px (Shift+arrow = 5 px). Click a handle on the chart to select it.
